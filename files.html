🧭 Quick Navigation & User Guide

1. How to Upload & Store Files

Click "+ Upload" or the floating + button on mobile. Choose any PDF, video, or image. It persists permanently in your browser & backend!

2. Small vs Large File Policies

• Small (< 5MB): Broadcast to ALL 9 nodes for instant read.
• Large (≥ 5MB): Sharded into 4MB chunks with Web Crypto SHA-256.

3. Download & Verify

Click "⬇️ Download" on any file card to reconstruct the verified file. Click "🔍 Verify" to test block hashes.

4. Mobile App Experience

On your smartphone, tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu to install Vault as a native app with zero lag!

Object Details

Upload & Multi-Node Distribution

Smart Placement Engine: Files < 5MB are broadcasted across ALL 9 nodes for instant availability. Files ≥ 5MB are partitioned into 4MB high-throughput chunks with multi-zone quorum replication.

⚡ Active Storage Policy
• Small File Threshold: 5.0 MB (9-Node Full Redundancy)
• Chunk Sharding Limit: 4.0 MB per block (SHA-256 Verified)
🔐 Step 1: Initializing cryptographic stream
⚖️ Step 2: Evaluating 5MB threshold & placement policy
🧩 Step 3: Generating Web Crypto SHA-256 checksums
🚀 Step 4: Committing replicas to Node Cluster (:9001 - :9009)
