Distributed object storage

Keep large objects alive across unreliable nodes.

Vault stores, replicates, retrieves, and repairs data while nodes fail independently. Concurrent puts and gets, configurable durability, integrity verification, and automatic replica repair — with predictable availability and low recovery time.

Objects

Put, get, delete

Chunked writes fan out to healthy nodes. Reads reassemble from a verified replica. Missing or corrupt chunks never silently succeed.

Repair

Scrub, heal, rebalance

Integrity scrubs checksum every replica. Under-replicated chunks copy from a healthy source onto a live target.
