🧭 Quick Navigation & User Guide

Step 1: Upload Files (File Manager)

Go to File Manager, click "+ Upload Document / Video". Files under 5MB are automatically broadcasted across all 9 nodes. Larger files are sharded into 4MB blocks with SHA-256 validation.

Step 2: Inspect 9 Storage Nodes (Cluster Health)

Open Cluster Health to see live metrics for Node Alpha through Node Iota (:9001 - :9009) and the physical replica distribution matrix.

Step 3: Test Real-Time Self-Healing (Chaos Lab)

Go to Chaos Lab, click "Kill Node" or "Inject Corruption". Watch the autonomous healer instantly recover missing replicas on surviving nodes!

Step 4: Use as Mobile App

On mobile, use the bottom navigation bar or tap "Add to Home Screen" to use Vault as a native app just like Google Drive.
